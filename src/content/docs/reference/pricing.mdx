## The cost lever

<div class="ecsx-lever not-content">
  <div class="ecsx-lever__col ecsx-lever__col--before">
    <span class="ecsx-lever__head">One ALB per service</span>
    <span class="ecsx-lever__big">25×</span>
    <span class="ecsx-lever__note">25 services → 25 ALBs to pay for and run</span>
  </div>
  <span class="ecsx-lever__arrow" aria-hidden="true">
    →
  </span>
  <div class="ecsx-lever__col ecsx-lever__col--after">
    <span class="ecsx-lever__head">One shared ALB</span>
    <span class="ecsx-lever__big">1×</span>
    <span class="ecsx-lever__note">up to 25 services share a single ALB</span>
  </div>
</div>

Sharing one ALB across many services (up to 25 per ALB) is the main cost lever versus one ALB per
service.

<Aside type="note">ACM public certificate pricing and ELB rates change over time — always check the current pages below before estimating.</Aside>

See [Amazon ECS pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/ecs/pricing/) and [Elastic Load Balancing pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/elasticloadbalancing/pricing/).
